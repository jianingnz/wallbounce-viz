When things collide — and the Δt ladder

Time from release to the first particle touching the floor or a wall, per episode (10,000 train episodes of fully_random_sand_fps1024, 10 ms bins). Vertical ticks: the training Δt ladder; the heavy tick is Δtmax = 150 ms, chosen ≤ the mean pre-collision time — one maximal step spans a typical flight-to-impact.

Data coverage per ladder rung (2 GT + 2 future frames)

Share of episodes whose settle-trimmed motion span fits a full N=2 window at each step size. Every rung except the largest covers all episodes; at 150 ms the 31% shortest episodes train only on the smaller rungs.

Effective episode length (motion only)

Settle-trimmed episode lengths (25 ms bins). Raw files are all 1.2 s; on average only the first 0.52 s contains motion — window placement and the ladder statistics use this effective span, the files themselves are untouched.
