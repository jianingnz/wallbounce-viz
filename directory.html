Measurements and studies the questions that shaped the models

pagewhat it answers
Out-of-range Δt Query a model at step sizes inside and beyond the range it trained on. Interpolation is free; extrapolation costs 12–50%. This is the measurement that motivated training over a wide Δt ladder at all.
Cube: 331 M vs. 39 M The same cube task at 5 ms on two models 8.5× apart in size, identical episodes and protocol — does capacity help here?
Narrowed Δt vs. the original The two sand models on the same eval set, both splits, over the step sizes both trained on.
One big step vs. AR rollout Reaching a physical instant in one large step, or by chaining 2.5 ms chunks — same sampler, same context.
Samplers on the training set One denoising step against twenty-five, and the pyramid schedule, on data the model has seen.
Δt design: collision timing How long contacts last, and what that implies for the largest usable step size.
Collision moments Ground-truth episodes with contact highlighted, and how much material is touching a wall over time.
FPS subsampling pilot Whether a fixed farthest-point subset of the 2,048 material points is faithful enough to train on. It is; every current model uses one.
Water-3D on this stack DeepMind’s liquid benchmark, first contact.
Δt study · Horizon rollout The earliest two viewers, on validation windows from the wall-bounce set.
