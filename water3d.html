Liquid, first contact

DeepMind's Water-3D — SPH water sloshing in a closed box — read into the particle world model by Robert Ren. Two questions so far: what does a checkpoint trained on solids do with liquid (nothing good), and can the architecture represent water at all when asked to memorise a single batch (yes, once you stop clipping the targets). Everything below is a fixed-batch overfit or a zero-shot receipt. No model has been trained on the water corpus yet.

A solids checkpoint meets water

Millimetres. Window error is one 16-frame prediction from 4 given frames; rollout error is 48 frames of the model feeding on itself. Water is 17× worse per window than the control.

It pours through the walls. At frame 48, 98% of the true particles are still inside the box and only 32% of the model's are. Water-3D ships no boundary particles and the domain adds no distance-to-wall feature, so a model that never saw liquid has no idea the container exists.

The clipping floor was doing the damage

Training clips each target to a frozen per-axis motion limit. Water's initial collapse moves far faster than those limits allow, so the target itself is truncated — and simply integrating the clipped ground truth already scores 26.1 mm. No model trained this way can score below that.

Window 0 is the initial release, and 19.7% of its targets are clipped. It alone carries the batch: arm A scores 88 mm on this window against a batch mean of 38.6.

Fitting the scale on the violent first window instead of all of them cost more than it saved (33.6 mm): the calmest window went from 9 to 21 mm under it. What this does not answer

  • No corpus training. The 1,000-trajectory training split has never been trained on; every number here is one batch or one zero-shot pass.
  • No held-out generalisation. An overfit fit says the architecture can represent liquid, nothing more.
  • Walls are still implicit. A model can learn this one box because every trajectory uses it, but nothing transfers to another container.
  • Each window sees 4,096 of up to 13,248 particles, so the model never sees the whole fluid; the rest act only through the sampled neighbours.
  • The UNet's coarse-point budget for water is unvalidated — every arm above ran at 256, inherited from sand, where the domain now suggests 1,024.
