Timestep-conditioned particle prediction

One trained model, queried at four different simulation step sizes. MOVi-spheres wall-bounce, held-out validation scenes. ← back to the training viewer

1. Setup

Every window gives the model 5 ground-truth context frames at the native 42 ms rate and asks it to generate the future from pure noise up to a matched physical horizon of ≈0.67 s. The fixed-Δt model can only step at 42 ms (16 steps). The implicit-Δt model takes the step durations as input (encoded through time-aware rotary attention) and is queried at 42, 84, 125 and 167 ms — so it reaches the same instant in 16, 8, 5 or 4 steps. 125 ms never occurred in training. Windows are wall-impact events: the first predicted step contains the ball–wall contact. Error = mean particle distance to ground truth over the horizon (ADE), in real meters (box width 10 m, ball Ø 0.7–1.4 m).
