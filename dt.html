Timestep-conditioned particle prediction

The same wall-bounce event, predicted at four different simulation step sizes by one model. MOVi-spheres, held-out validation scenes. ← back to the training viewer

1. Setup

Each example gives the models 5 ground-truth context frames at the native 42 ms rate; the future is then generated from pure noise up to a matched physical horizon of ≈0.67 s. The fixed-Δt model can only step at 42 ms (16 steps). The implicit-Δt model receives the step durations as input — encoded through time-aware rotary attention — and is queried at 42, 84, 125 and 167 ms, reaching the same instant in 16, 8, 5 or 4 steps; 125 ms never occurred in training. All panes below play on one physical clock (10× slow motion): coarse settings advance in fewer, longer strides and arrive at the same moment. Error = mean particle distance to ground truth over the horizon (ADE), real meters (box width 10 m, ball Ø 0.7–1.4 m).
