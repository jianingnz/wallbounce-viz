Autoregressive rollouts of training episodes the model has already seen, at every step size it was trained on, next to the held-out split. Two inference schemes: a single denoising step, and the full 25.

The same rollouts in 3D

Truth beside both predictions, from training episodes. A single denoising step returns the conditional mean, so the thing to check is whether it quietly shrinks the object — on the cube model it collapses to 0.45× true size at 150 ms. Measured here, it does not: the sand predictions stay within 2 % of the truth's size at every step size, for both samplers. On this model the two really are interchangeable, in shape as well as in error.
