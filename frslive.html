Stress case — corner splash

A wall-throw that splashes into a corner: two wall planes and the floor engage at once — the hardest physics in this corpus, and the place where the nearest-wall feature's argmin switches between walls. Tracked at every checkpoint since it was flagged.

Four scenarios × every step size

The same four episodes — a corner splash, a wall throw, a drop and a slam — each predicted from 2 ground-truth frames all the way to the end of the episode, at every step size in the ladder. Rollout ADE in mm; blank = the episode is too short to hold a window at that step.

Lower is better; the best step size for each scenario is highlighted. Fine steps take many more chained chunks to cover the same span, so their errors accumulate — coarse steps cover the episode in a handful of predictions.
