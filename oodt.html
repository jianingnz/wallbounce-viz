Sand model trained on Δt ∈ {2.5, 5, 10} ms — queried at step sizes inside and beyond that range. Error at matched physical time (same 297 val windows, context 5 × 2.5 ms GT frames, one window of 16 steps per curve).

Penalty relative to the best in-range Δt at the same instant

Inside the trained range the curves lie on top of each other — including 7.5 ms, which was never trained but interpolates (dashed blue). Beyond the range (15 / 20 ms, orange/red) the same physical instant costs 12–50 % more error, and the gap widens with time. This measurement motivated training the wide-Δt models.
