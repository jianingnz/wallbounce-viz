A sphere of sand and a hollow cube, thrown at a wall

Two freshly simulated 2,048-point episodes from the updated generator. The sand object is now a sphere rather than a rectangular box; the elastic cube is unchanged. Each scene plays out in real physical time — slow it down to see the impact.

Particle world model · meeting of Sept. 8, 2026

Every episode in the dataset starts the same way: one object is placed at a random pose inside a one-metre arena — a floor and four walls with Coulomb friction 0.5 — given an initial velocity and a spin, and then left to the simulator for up to 1.2 seconds. Positions of all 2,048 material points are recorded every 2.5 ms. When nothing moves anymore the clip is cut.

Below, the two objects that make up the new sand-sphere variant of the set. Both episodes were drawn from the same seed, so they start from the same pose, the same throw and the same spin — only the material differs. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. Only the walls behind the object are drawn, so the view is never blocked.
